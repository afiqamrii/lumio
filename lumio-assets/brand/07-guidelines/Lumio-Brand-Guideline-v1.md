# Lumio Brand Guideline v1

26 September 2026. Design baseline. This Markdown version contains the core rules from the illustrated PDF.

## Brand idea

Lumio is a playful educational game for children aged 5-9. Learning makes their world brighter. The experience should be warm, curious, polished and trustworthy. Tagline: Play. Learn. Glow.

## Personality

Playful, thoughtful and confident. Keep lessons readable, show meaningful progress and provide natural stopping points. Encourage effort. Avoid harsh failure language, pressure to return and a cluttered interface.

## Logo family

Primary: integrated-o logo. Compact branding: approved wordmark A. Small UI: simplified dot alternate. Launcher/avatar: approved turquoise wink icon. Do not replace the custom wordmark with typed text. Earlier side-by-side and stacked layouts are superseded exploration files.

## Logo handling

Proposed clear space: 0.25 times the o height beyond all visible artwork. Provisional test widths: integrated logo 240 px, wordmark 120 px, small-UI alternate 80 px. These are not validated minimum sizes. Preserve aspect ratio and approved composition. Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, recolor or place over busy content. Dark and monochrome variants remain pending.

## Color

The exact 12-color palette and four functional shades live in brand/05-colors/lumio-colors.json and .css. White on violet 5.36:1; plum on gold 9.65:1; plum on turquoise 6.65:1. Do not use white normal-size text on gold or turquoise. Ratios apply to exact solid sRGB tokens, not to artwork samples. This is not a whole-product accessibility audit.

## Typography

Recommended: Nunito 700-800 for display, Lexend 400-600 for Latin learning/UI text, Noto Sans Arabic 400-600 for Arabic. No font binaries are bundled and no runtime shaping test has been performed. Starting sizes: headings 32/40 px; section titles 24/32; child instructions 20/30; controls 18/26; parent body 16/24; metadata 14/20. Test confusing glyphs, math symbols, diacritics and text scaling. Custom logo is separate. Sources [1]-[3].

## Character

Nine reference expressions: happy, excited, curious, thinking, surprised, proud, confused, sleepy and celebrating. Expression sheet and usage JSON exist. Nine individual reaction images are still pending. The sheet is not a runtime sprite atlas. Preserve crown direction, face proportions, outline weight, belly and tail identity. Do not use expressions to shame or pressure a child.

## Illustration and worlds

Use simple rounded shapes, consistent contours and flat/cel color. Number Kingdom: exact countable objects and geometry. Word Woods: readable letter and story objects. Language Village: reviewed everyday Bahasa Melayu examples. Arabic Oasis: respectful culturally reviewed scenes and accurate script. Logic Lab: clear mechanisms and cause/effect. Discovery Island: factually accurate nature/science. These are art directions, not finished world assets.

## Motion

Proposed timings: button press 80-120 ms; answer feedback 200-350 ms; transitions 220-320 ms; Lumi reactions 500-900 ms; milestone flourish 800-1200 ms. Keep hit areas stable and settle after reactions. Reduced motion uses immediate states or brief fades. No camera shake, strobing or blocking celebration loops. No animation files created.

## Sound and voice

Suggested audio only: startup 0.5-0.8 s ascending motif; correct 0.15-0.3 s warm pluck; retry 0.1-0.2 s neutral tap; reward 0.4-0.8 s rising motif; transition 0.1-0.2 s airy sweep; Lumi vocals 0.2-0.6 s. Avoid buzzer/shaming sounds. Provide independent sound controls and avoid stacked sounds. No audio files exist. Copy: You found it! Good try. Let’s look again. What do you notice? Ready for a little break?

## Product expression

Child UI: one clear task, short instructions, large choices, clear progress; 48 x 48 logical-pixel controls are a proposed starting point. Parent UI: calm white/mist, labeled progress charts and plain language. Pair icons/text with color, support sound-off, reduced motion, text scaling, focus access and Arabic RTL. Contrast pairs use W3C guidance [4], not a whole-product compliance claim. Provide natural stops and parent-controlled purchasing.

## Status and references

Proposed rules in this guide require implementation review.
